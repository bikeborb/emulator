### perf(vulkan): wake the worker thread less often on Android

Every hand-over of recorded commands wakes the worker with a futex call. A 32 KB chunk filled
every twenty draws or so and FlushWork handed over every 8 draws on top: 26,000 wake-ups a
second in Red Dead Redemption. On Android chunks are 128 KB and FlushWork hands over every 64
draws. Desktop is unchanged.
