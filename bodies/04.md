### fix(shader_recompiler): follow 16-bit texture table lookups

DOOM Eternal reads its texture handle through a table: a 16-bit entry of one constant buffer,
indexed by a field of another. The texture pass did not know GetCbufU16, walked into its
arguments and bound the index word as the handle, so most surfaces sampled the wrong texture.

The pass now tracks the table lookup (constant index, shift and base), keeps shift and mask of
the fields it follows, and the pipelines resolve the handle through the table at bind time.
Menus render correctly. Lookups whose index is computed in the shader are still open.

Review fixes folded in:

Texture handles:
- The compute path checks the table entry against the size of the
  table's constant buffer, as the graphics path does.
- A shift or bit field taken after two halves were combined, or after a
  table entry was read, is passed over as before instead of being
  applied to the word.
- A mask on a table entry that keeps all 16 bits changes nothing; any
  other is passed over. A mask on a field already taken out of the word
  applies at the field's position.
