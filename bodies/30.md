### fix(texture_cache): shrink copy extents to both mips and whole blocks

MakeShrinkImageCopies used the destination mip as the copy extent, but
the overlap was found by address alone, so its mip can be smaller. The
extent is now what both mips have. A block-compressed region that is
neither whole blocks nor the end of the subresource on both images
(vkCmdCopyImage srcImage-01728/01729, dstImage-01732/01733) holds less
than one block of one image and is skipped. CopyImage returns early on
an empty list.

### fix(texture_cache): alias copy extents in texels of the source image

Alias copies used copy_size as the VkImageCopy extent. It counts blocks
of a compressed image and texels of an uncompressed one, while the
extent is in texels of the copy's source image, so a compressed source
copied a block count as texels (2 of 8 texels of a 2x2-block BC3 mip).
Each alias list now gets its own extent from its source image; when
both images are compressed it must also fit the other image and be
whole blocks or reach the subresource end on both
(srcImage-01728/01729, dstImage-01732/01733), else that mip is skipped.
