### perf(buffer_cache): write uploads straight into unified memory buffers

On integrated GPUs with a DEVICE_LOCAL | HOST_VISIBLE | HOST_COHERENT
memory type, buffer cache buffers are allocated persistently mapped. Each
buffer keeps one GPU-use tick per 4 KiB page; an upload whose pages the GPU
has finished with (plus one tick of margin for a flush between stamp and
record) is read straight into the buffer, everything else goes through
staging and a GPU copy as before. The direct and staged paths share one read
loop. The rule is a constexpr function in buffer_cache/uma.h with unit tests.
Discrete GPUs are unaffected. On the Thor about 82% of uploads go direct;
frame rate and GPU busy are unchanged in the four test games.
