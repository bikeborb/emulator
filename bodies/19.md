### chore(common): use the standard library where C++20 has it

Common::BitCast called std::bit_cast whenever __cpp_lib_bit_cast was
defined, which it is with every compiler this builds with; the 14 files
that used it call std::bit_cast and include <bit>.
Common::make_unique_for_overwrite had the three forms of
std::make_unique_for_overwrite; scratch_buffer.h calls the standard one.
Common::DerivedFrom, ConvertibleTo and IsIntegral were local versions of
std::derived_from, std::convertible_to and std::integral with TODOs to
replace them; their four users use the standard concepts. The old
DerivedFrom was a requires expression of two simple requirements, which
only asks that the expressions are valid, so it accepted every pair of
types; std::derived_from checks what it says.
