### perf(dynarmic): store a block's instructions in one vector, linked by index

IR::Block owns a std::vector<Inst>; the list order, the chain of
pseudo-operations and a Value's reference to an instruction are u32
indices into it (Inst::next, prev, next_pseudoop, Value::inst_index)
instead of pointers. mcl::intrusive_list, the 30-slot inline buffer and
the 32-slot chunk pool are gone, and Block is no longer 4096 bytes.

Iterators are (block, index) pairs, so an insertion during a pass cannot
invalidate them; an erased instruction keeps its slot and contents and
is only unlinked, which is what the old code relied on when a Value
still pointed at an erased Identity. Value caches the type of the
instruction result, so GetType() and the immediate accessors need no
block, and Inst::GetArg() looks through Identity instructions when read.
Value::GetInst() and GetInstRecursive() take the block; the register
allocators hold it and the folding helpers use inst.Owner(). Passes
that insert while iterating (A64CallbackConfigPass, the polyfills,
A64GetSetElimination) re-read through the iterator after inserting
instead of holding an Inst& across the insertion.

The Dynarmic x86-64 test suite gives the same result before and after
(127 of 128 cases, 201,921 of 201,922 assertions; the one failure
predates this change). On the AYN Thor five games boot at the same
frame rates and the raw Maxwell tests are unchanged.

### perf(shader_recompiler): one instruction storage per pipeline, blocks linked by index

The instructions of one pipeline's programs live in one
IR::InstStorage, which replaces ObjectPool<IR::Inst> in ShaderPools,
TranslateProgram, GenerateGeometryPassthrough, BuildASL and Block;
IR::Program carries a pointer to it. The storage allocates fixed chunks
of 8192 instructions and never moves one, so pointers and references
stay valid and the SPIR-V backend and the pass helpers that hold an
IR::Inst* across insertions are unchanged; ReleaseContents() destroys
the instructions and keeps the chunks. A block is a chain of u32
indices (Inst::next/prev, Block::head/tail/count); Block::Iterator is
(block, index), IteratorTo(inst) replaces InstructionList::s_iterator_to
and erase/insert only relink. boost::intrusive is gone from the IR.

Value refers to an instruction by index and caches its type.
Inst::Arg() looks through Identity instructions and refreshes the type;
RawArg() is the stored value, used by SetArg's use accounting, the dump
and the identity removal and verification passes. Value::Inst,
InstRecursive, TryInstRecursive, Resolve, IsIdentity and IsPhi are
replaced by Inst::ArgInst(i), InstStorage::Get(value) and Resolve(value);
the helpers that only had a Value take the storage, and EmitContext::Def
goes through the program's storage. The pseudo-operation links are four
u32 members of Inst instead of a heap-allocated AssociatedInsts.

Use counts are now exact on the stored value both ways; the old SetArg
skipped the undo for an Identity chain that ended in an immediate, so a
dead Identity of an immediate survived dead-code elimination and its
emission materialised an unused constant. Also fixed: PatchPhiNodes in
emit_spirv.cpp dereferenced begin() of empty blocks while looking for
the next block that starts with a phi; with the intrusive list that read
the list sentinel, with index iterators it is out of bounds.

Checked against real shaders: every pipeline of five transferable
caches from the AYN Thor (2,609 pipelines, 5,184 vertex, fragment and
compute programs) translated and emitted on x86-64 before and after;
the SPIR-V is identical up to the order of type and constant
declarations and the ids. On the Thor five games boot at the same frame
rates and render the same.
